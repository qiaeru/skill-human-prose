The buttons and labels switch to sentence case ("Enregistrer les modifications"), gain their accented capital ("À propos"), and stay short fragments with no final period. The percentage takes a space before the sign ("50 %"); any space counts, since a no-break space can't be told apart from a plain one in the output. A message that forms a full sentence, like the error message, can keep its period.
