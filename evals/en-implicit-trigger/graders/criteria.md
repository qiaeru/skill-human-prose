The response returns the corrected email, with the short sentences joined, by a connective where the email gives the link and by plain coordination elsewhere, and loses no information (Thursday, the postponed migration, two hours of downtime, customers to warn). The outage explains why customers must be warned, so that link can be written, while nothing opposes the Thursday deploy to the postponed migration, so a "but" or a "while" between the two invents a link.

It doesn't attribute the decision to a third party the email never names (management, a client, a vendor). Keeping the passive, switching to a "we" that means the sender, or flagging the gap to the author are all acceptable.

It keeps the greeting and the sign-off.
