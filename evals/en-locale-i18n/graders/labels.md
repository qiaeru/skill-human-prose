The button labels follow one capitalization convention (all Title Case or all sentence case, not a mix), and short fragments such as "About us" carry no final period. A string that forms a full sentence or a question (the error message, the quota message, the delete confirmation, the onboarding text) keeps its final punctuation, and an exclamation that greets ("Welcome!") is acceptable. Judge only the JSON values; commentary outside the file doesn't count.
