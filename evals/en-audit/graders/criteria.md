The response is a numbered list of tells, not a rewrite of the text. Each entry quotes the passage, gives the fix in a few words, and marks a priority, high or low.

The list catches at least the "In a world where" opener, the "doesn't just X; it Y" contrast, the ad-copy fragments, the em dash, the inflated significance ("paves the way," "the future looks bright"), and the borrowed authority ("Experts agree").

The response gives no numeric score and doesn't say whether a person or a machine wrote the text. It offers to fix the numbers the user picks.
